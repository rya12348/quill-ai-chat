What’s a good way to start learning the piano as an adult?

Start with ten focused minutes a day rather than an hour on Sundays. Three things to do in week one:

  1. Learn the C major scale with correct fingering, hands separately.
  2. Pick one song you love and learn its first four bars.
  3. Record yourself once a week so you can hear progress.
